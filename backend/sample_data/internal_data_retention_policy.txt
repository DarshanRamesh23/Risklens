INTERNAL RISK POLICY: DATA RETENTION AND VENDOR HANDLING (v3)

Section 1 — Scope
This policy applies to all third-party vendors that store, process, or transmit
company data, including customer PII, on behalf of the company.

Section 2 — Encryption Requirements
2.1 All vendor systems storing PII must encrypt data at rest using AES-256 or
equivalent.
2.2 All data in transit between vendor and company systems must use TLS 1.2 or
higher. Vendors using TLS 1.0 or 1.1 are considered non-compliant and must
remediate within 90 days of assessment.

Section 3 — Incident History
3.1 Vendors with two or more reportable security incidents in the trailing 24
months are classified as elevated risk and require quarterly re-assessment
rather than annual.
3.2 A reportable incident includes any unauthorized access to PII, regardless
of whether data exfiltration was confirmed.

Section 4 — Data Retention and Deletion
4.1 Vendors must delete company data within 30 days of contract termination
and provide written certification of deletion.
4.2 Vendors retaining backups beyond 30 days post-termination must disclose
backup retention schedules and encryption status of those backups.

Section 5 — Sub-processors
5.1 Vendors must disclose all sub-processors with access to company data.
5.2 Use of undisclosed sub-processors is treated as a material contract breach.
